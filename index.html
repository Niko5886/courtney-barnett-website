About Courtney Barnett

Courtney Barnett is an Australian singer, songwriter, and musician known for her deadpan vocal style and witty, fragmented lyrics. Born in 1987, she has established herself as one of Australia's most successful musical export artists. Her career began with the release of several EPs, such as "I've Got a Friend Called Emily Ferris" (2012) and "How to Carve a Carrot into a Rose" (2013), which gained a cult following.

Courtney Barnett is known for her rock trio sound, sharp lyrics, and conversational singing style. Based in Melbourne, she is recognized as an important voice in indie rock music, successfully blending slightly rough rock with folk and garage sounds. She also founded her own label, Milk! Records, which is key to the release of her music.

Live Performances

Before her solo career, she was part of the psych-country band Immigrant Union (2011-2013), where she also actively participated with slide guitar. Her career includes numerous tours in the US, Europe, and Australia, playing an important role in the contemporary music scene and making an impact with her unique approach to lyrics and performance.

Notable Collaborations

Her most famous collaboration is with American musician Kurt Vile. They released a joint album, Lotta Sea Lice, in 2017, featuring original songs and covers of both artists' songs. Their collaboration began with mutual respect and gradually developed into a full-length album featuring other musicians from the tour and studio. This work received positive reviews for its spontaneous and authentic sound.

Discography

Studio Albums: Courtney Barnett's discography includes acclaimed releases such as "Sometimes I Sit and Think, and Sometimes I Just Sit" (2015), which showcased her distinctive songwriting and quickly established her as a major force in indie rock. Her follow-up album "Lotta Sea Lice" (2017) with Kurt Vile demonstrated her versatility and collaborative spirit.

Latest Works: Continuing to evolve her sound, her recent albums explore deeper themes while maintaining the authenticity that defines her career. Her music is available on all major streaming platforms, and her records have received critical acclaim for their honest and introspective approach to songwriting.
